Genomics Workflow Microbials Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026

Genomics Workflow Microbials (GWM) is a free, open-source desktop app for Windows, Mac and Linux that types bacterial isolates from sequencing reads. It is made by TinusTK ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app keeps on your computer and what it sends over the internet. The app's source code is public at github.com/TinusTK/GenomicsWorkflowMicrobials, so everything here can be checked.

In short: GWM has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. We run no server, and nothing about you or your work is sent to us. Your sequencing reads, sample names, results and workflows stay on your computer. The app only contacts other websites to download things it needs: its own updates, the tools' containers, reference databases and tools you add. Those websites see your IP address, like any download.

1. What stays on your computer

  • Your data: sequencing reads, sample names, results, run records, workflows you build, tools you add and reference databases. They are read and written only on your computer, in the folders listed under Settings › Where things are kept.
  • The analysis itself: every tool runs in a container on your own computer. No reads or results are uploaded anywhere.
  • The app's local service: the app talks to its own engine through an address that only works on your computer (127.0.0.1). It is not reachable from the network unless you start the engine yourself from the command line with the option that opens it.

2. What the app downloads, and from where

These requests carry no information about you or your samples. Each website sees your IP address and the usual technical details of a download (such as the program making the request).

3. What the app never does

  • It does not send your reads, sample names, results, file names or folder names to us or to anyone else.
  • It has no usage tracking, crash reporting, advertising or analytics.
  • It does not ask for, or store, a name, email address or account.

4. Problem reports and contacting us

When a run fails you can save a problem report. It is a text file saved on your computer, and it is only sent if you choose to send it yourself. To keep it safe to share, the app leaves sequence data out of it and replaces sample names and your user name with placeholders. If you email us or open an issue on GitHub, we receive what you write and use it only to answer you and to fix the problem. Issues on GitHub are public.

5. Changes to your computer

The app's tools run in Docker. Setting it up, starting and stopping it, and changing how much memory the tools may use, are done from buttons in the app that say what they do. One thing the app does by itself: on Windows, when no memory amount is set for the Linux side of Windows (WSL), it sets it to the computer's memory minus 4 GB the first time it starts, and shows a message saying so. Closing the app with Quit switches Docker off (on Windows the Linux side with it), unless a run is still going. Settings › Remove everything shows what the app keeps on your computer and removes what you choose.

6. Children

GWM is a tool for laboratory and research work and is not directed at children.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app starts to use the internet in a new way, this page is updated first and the date at the top changes. Earlier versions are kept in the website's public history.
