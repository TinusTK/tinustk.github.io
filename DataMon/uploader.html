DataMon Uploader

Uses YouTube API Services · Made by Tinus Christian Creeper

DataMon Uploader is a small private command-line tool that uploads the official trailers and Shorts for the game DataMon to the DataMon channel on YouTube. It is used only by the channel's owner on their own computer. It is not sold, offered to the public or used with anyone else's account.

What it does

  • Uploads a video file from the owner's computer to the owner's own channel, with a title, description, tags, category and privacy setting (videos.insert).
  • Optionally sets a custom thumbnail on that video (thumbnails.set).
  • Shows which channel it is signed in to and lists that channel's latest uploads, so the owner can check them (channels.list, playlistItems.list, videos.list).

It does not read, store or analyse other channels, comments, viewers or analytics, and it shows no YouTube content to anyone else.

How it signs in

The owner signs in once with Google (OAuth 2.0) in their browser and picks the DataMon channel. Google gives the tool a token that is saved only on the owner's computer. The owner can remove the tool's access at any time on Google's security settings page.
