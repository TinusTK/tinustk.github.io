Raise a DataMon.

A virtual pet game for iPhone and Android. Hatch a Datatama, care for it through seven stages, and let your care, training and battles decide what it becomes.

Download on the App Store Join the Android closed beta Android is in closed testing on Google Play. Ask to join on our Discord.

The game

Ten DataMon lines, each with its own evolutions, hidden routes and secret dragon forms, drawn in HD with idle and attack animations.

Care decides everything

Two DataVs, each with its own Pet. Feed it, train it, clean up after it and put it to bed. What you do, and what you skip, shapes the route it evolves down.

  • Food and strength hearts, effort, sickness and injuries
  • Training mini games that build Power
  • Evo Crystals, Holy Ray and other items from the Shop

Battle your way up

Fight through ten Adventure areas, battle your own Pets against each other, or take them online. Every battle plays out at 60 fps, and the winning blow lands with a knockout blast.

Online

Connect to the DataMon Public Server, or battle friends on the same Wi-Fi. iPhones and Android phones play together.

World Boss

A new legendary boss every week. Everyone fights the same one, and everyone who helps bring it down is rewarded.

Mini Bosses

Smaller bosses that keep coming. Land the Final Blow for the biggest share of Shekel.

Tournaments

Daily and weekly. Beat the Champion to take the spot, and hold it until the Tournament ends to win the jackpot.

PVP rooms

Open a room, or join one. Put Shekel on the line in a wager battle if you're feeling brave.

Retire them to the Farm

On the Farm your Pets live, train and sometimes fight each other. One becomes the Alpha. The treasure chest fills with Celestial Shekel while you're away, even with the app closed.

Petting a sad Pet cheers it up and earns you 10 Shekel.

Every Shekel counted

The Celestial Shekel Log keeps Total Earned, Total Loss and what's left pinned at the top. Each day lists where your Shekel came from, and where they went: the Shop, clock speeds, lost wagers.

The Farm Log shows what happened while you were away: moods, Farm Fights and who's the Alpha now.

The Farm

Every Farm building does a job. Let the Farm look after your Pets on its own, or raise them yourself on a DataV for more Power.

Let the Farm run itself

Send a Pet to the Farm and it's cared for automatically. It eats at the Restaurant, sleeps at the Hotel, trains at the Gym, builds Vital Values at Vital Health and gets patched up at the Hospital and Med-Bay. Its poops go to the Poop Area for Dung Roll and Spice.

The treasure chest fills with Celestial Shekel while you're away, even with the app closed, and every building levels up as it's used.

Or raise them yourself

A Pet you raise by hand on a DataV grows stronger. Every hatch and evolution there adds Bonus Power, up to about +228 over a full line, and its big evolutions bring a DataPerk pick.

On the Farm a Pet keeps the Power it has but stops earning more. Raise your best fighters yourself, and let the Farm care for the rest.

You can still jump in on the Farm: cook dishes at the Restaurant for a Power boost, tuck sleepy Pets in at the Hotel, zap germs at the Hospital.

Spice

Your Farm Pets' poops become Compost, Compost becomes Spice, and Spice makes Pets evolve sooner.

Brew Spice in the Poop Area

Pour Compost into a Spice Pod and watch the sand run through the hourglass. Six hours later a glowing Spice drops onto the floor and waits there until you tap it. One pod to start, up to five as the Poop Area levels up.

Give a Farm Pet the job on the Spice Farm and it keeps the pods filled for you. It can't battle while it works.

Spice Wars

Every DataPlayer fights the same desert boss, the Scorpion King, and chips away at one shared HP bar. It stays up until it falls, then a new one arrives at 8pm Darwin time.

Stage VI+ Pets only, one on one and turn by turn, with no misses. Watch the timeline: the boss Enrages in waves, and from the third Enrage its hits can knock your Pet out.

  • The Top 5 by damage win the most Spice
  • Everyone who fought gets Spice, Chef Coins and Shekel
  • The Final Blow earns extra Shekel

DataPerks

Passive upgrades each Pet collects as it grows. Pick one of three cards, and it stays with that Pet through every evolution after.

Pick 1 of 3

A Pet gets a pick when it evolves into Stage III, IV or V (a Minor DataPerk) and Stage VI or VI+ (a Major one) on a DataV. Beating the Adventure Area 5 and Area 10 bosses for the first time gives one more of each.

  • Every pick deals a battle card and a life card when it can
  • A Pet never gets the same DataPerk twice
  • Most work everywhere, online battles too; a few only in one kind of fight (the Bracket Cup, bosses or the Gauntlet). DataPerks add up to +12% hit chance at most
  • Once a Pet has lived on the Farm it only earns the Adventure picks
  • An Enraged World Boss or Mini Boss carries 2 battle DataPerks of its own

DataDex

Register your Pets in the DataDex and they leave for good. In return, every form you fill in makes you stronger.

Fill in 80 forms

Farm Lands opens at DataPlayer level 25. Register a living Pet from a DataV or the Farm, and its form shows up in the DataDex.

Every form gives one bonus for good: Power, more Shekel earned, more DataPlayer EXP or a faster Farm chest. Higher stages give more.

Finish a whole line for 5,000 Shekel, 1,000 DataPlayer EXP and that line's own buff, from a better hit chance to cheaper Shop items.

Make it yours

DataMon is made to be shared.

Your own Pets. Add Pets from pictures or GIFs, plus DataPlayers, wallpapers, food and Farm buildings. They live, evolve and battle like any other.

Share packages. Export your creations as a content package from Content, send the .zip to a friend, and they import it from Content › Import.

Widget and backup. A home-screen widget shows your Pets, and your game backs up to iCloud.

Steps to Vital Values. Optional, read-only Health access turns your steps into care for your Pet.
