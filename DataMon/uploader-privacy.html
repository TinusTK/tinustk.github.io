DataMon Uploader Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

DataMon Uploader ("the tool") is a private command-line tool made by Tinus Christian Creeper ("we", "us") that uploads videos for the game DataMon to the DataMon channel. It is described on the DataMon Uploader page. This policy explains what the tool does with information.

In short: the tool runs only on the channel owner's own computer and talks only to Google. It has no server, sends nothing to us or anyone else, and keeps no data about anyone except the owner's own sign-in token on that computer.

1. YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services to upload videos and read the owner's own channel and uploads. By using the tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

2. Information the tool uses

  • Google sign-in token (OAuth 2.0): after the owner signs in with Google, the token is saved in a file on the owner's computer so they don't have to sign in for every upload. It is never sent anywhere except to Google to make API requests.
  • Channel name and ID: read from YouTube to show the owner which channel uploads go to.
  • The owner's videos and their details (file, title, description, tags, privacy, thumbnail): sent to YouTube when the owner uploads, and the latest uploads' titles, links and privacy are read back to show them to the owner.

The tool does not collect information about viewers, other channels, comments or analytics. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising.

3. How information is stored and shared

  • Everything stays on the owner's computer; the tool has no database or server of its own.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer any information obtained through YouTube API Services to anyone.
  • Channel and video details read from YouTube are shown on screen and not stored.

4. Removing access and deleting data

  • Revoke the tool's access to your Google account at any time on https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Delete the saved sign-in by deleting the tool's token.json file; nothing else is kept.
  • Videos uploaded with the tool live on YouTube and can be changed or deleted in YouTube Studio.
  • If you have any question about data and the tool, contact us and we'll answer or delete it within 7 days.

5. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
